# Tyll demo: 15 minute test with a hotel manager

Only real people can tell whether the demo is clear. Run this with two or three hotel or F&B managers who have not seen Tyll. Use a phone for tasks 1 and 2, and a laptop for the rest.

## How to run it

1. Open the demo at the welcome screen. Press **Enter demo** for them.
2. Say: "This is an early version with made up numbers. Please think out loud. If you get stuck, that is our problem, not yours."
3. Read one task at a time. Do not point or hint. Time each task, and count the wrong taps.
4. Reset the demo afterwards: Settings, then **Reset demo data**.

## Tasks

| # | Say this | Done when | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "A waiter just dropped 3 wine glasses at Maison Lune. Record it." | The Undo message appears with 3 wine glasses | Under 15 seconds |
| 2 | "Two kilos of chicken went off in the kitchen. Record it." | The Undo message appears with chicken breast | Under 15 seconds |
| 3 | "Which items are about to run out?" | They name chicken breast, or the critical list | Under 10 seconds |
| 4 | "What do you need to order today, and what will it cost?" | They reach Predictions and read the total for today | Under 20 seconds |
| 5 | "Create the order so someone can approve it." | The draft order opens | Under 20 seconds |
| 6 | "How much did the hotel make this week?" | They read the last 7 days on Revenue | Under 10 seconds |
| 7 | "Is the bar pouring too much wine anywhere?" | They reach Drinks control and name an outlet | Under 30 seconds |

## Ask at the end

- What did you think this product does?
- What was confusing or felt like too much?
- Which number would you not trust, and why?
- Would you open this every morning? What is missing for that?

## Scoring

Write down time and wrong taps for each task. Any task that two people fail, or that goes over its target, is the next thing to fix. Write down their words as well as the numbers.
